06 • Our Journey

From Sthapana to Visarjan

స్థాపన నుండి నిమజ్జనం వరకు — Eleven days of sacred devotion

DAY 01

Sthapana • స్థాపన

Welcoming Lord Ganesha with sacred Pranaprathishta, flower offerings, and the first Maha Aarti.

DAYS 02-04

Daily Pooja • నిత్య పూజ

Morning and evening rituals, Shodashopachara pooja, bhajans, and rhythmic circle of aarti lamps.

DAYS 05-10

Celebrations • ఉత్సవాలు

Cultural programs, youth devotional performances, community laddu auctions, and grand Maha Annadanam.

FINAL DAY

Visarjan • నిమజ్జనం

Grand farewell procession carrying Bappa to holy waters with traditional drums and devotional music.
